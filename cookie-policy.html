Cookie Policy

How pedgi.com uses cookies and similar technologies, and how you can control them.

The short version

We don’t use advertising, analytics or social media tracking cookies. The only third-party service that may set cookies is Google Maps, and it only loads if you allow it.

Last updated: November 19, 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as your browser’s local storage, work the same way. They can be used to remember settings or, on some websites, to track visitors.

What this website stores

When you choose an option in our cookie banner, we save that choice in your browser’s local storage under the name pedgi-cookie-consent, so we don’t ask again on every page. It contains only your choice and the date, never any personal or medical information, and it is kept for 12 months. This is strictly necessary for the banner to work.

Third-party services

  • Google Maps. Our office maps are provided by Google. If you select Accept all, or click Load map on an individual map, Google Maps loads and Google may set its own cookies. If you select Essential only, the maps stay hidden and you can open each location directly in Google Maps instead. See how Google uses cookies and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Fonts and page files. Some fonts, icons and styling files are delivered by Google Fonts and the jsDelivr content network. These do not set cookies, but like any web server they receive your IP address in order to send the files.
  • Online payments. The Make a Payment link takes you to Phreesia, a separate website with its own privacy and cookie policies.

Changing your choice

You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page. You can also clear or block cookies and site data in your browser’s settings; the website will still work, and maps will simply stay hidden.

Questions

If you have any questions about this policy, please call our office at (813) 870-4438.
