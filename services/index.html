Conditions We Treat & Clinical Focus Areas

From everyday tummy aches to complex digestive and growth disorders, our team delivers advanced diagnostics and gentle, personalized treatment plans tailored to each child.

Pediatric Abdominal Pain

Acute, Recurrent & Functional

Evaluation and evidence-based relief for acute stomach aches, recurrent abdominal pain, irritable bowel syndrome (IBS), functional dyspepsia, and chronic stomach distress in children of all ages.

  • Non-invasive diagnostic protocols
  • Gut-brain axis & dietary therapies
  • Targeted pediatric pharmacotherapy
Explore Abdominal Pain Care

Pediatric GI Bleeding

Urgent & Chronic Evaluation

Rapid and thorough assessment for rectal bleeding, blood in stool, vomiting blood, juvenile polyps, anal fissures, eosinophilic conditions, and inflammatory bowel diseases (Crohn's & Colitis).

  • Pediatric endoscopy & colonoscopy
  • Comprehensive stool & blood testing
  • Polyp removal and bleeding site care
Explore GI Bleeding Care

Acid Reflux & GERD

Infants, Children & Teens

Specialized evaluation for infant spitting up, painful swallowing, heartburn, chronic coughing, arching during feeds, poor weight gain, and gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD).

  • Infant feeding & formula optimization
  • Reflux monitoring & pH impedance
  • Pediatric-dosed medical management
Explore Acid Reflux & GERD Care

Nutritional Disorders

Growth, Feeding & Absorption

Targeted clinical support for failure to thrive, nutrient malabsorption, celiac disease, food protein allergies, micronutrient deficiencies, and feeding difficulties.

  • Pediatric growth curve tracking
  • Celiac & malabsorption workups
  • Custom clinical nutrition plans
Explore Nutritional Care
