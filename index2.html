What we treat

Tummy troubles, solved with care

From fussy feeding in infancy to inflammatory bowel disease in the teen years, we diagnose and treat the full range of digestive, liver and nutrition concerns in children.

Care at every age

From first bottle to college years

Children aren’t small adults. Our care is built around how kids grow — so the approach for a newborn looks very different from the one for a busy teenager.

Infants 0–1 yr

Reflux, formula intolerance, feeding difficulties and weight gain.

Children 1–12 yrs

Tummy aches, constipation, diarrhea and growth concerns.

Teens & young adults 13–21 yrs

Crohn’s, colitis, liver conditions and nutrition — with privacy and respect.

Your first visit

Getting started is easy

We know a specialist visit can feel like a big step. Here’s what to expect — no surprises.

  1. Call or get a referral

    Reach us at (813) 870-4438, or ask your pediatrician to send a referral.

  2. Complete your forms

    Download our patient forms and check your insurance ahead of time.

  3. Meet your specialist

    We’ll listen, examine your child gently and walk you through a clear care plan.

Urgent requests are typically seen within 2–5 business days.
